uMap → Walks Map converter

Convert a fresh uMap GeoJSON export or complete uMap backup JSON into the walks-data.geojson file used by your GitHub walking map. Your selected file is processed in this browser; it is not uploaded to a server.

The converter reads years from the uMap yearly layers or each walk's date. It reads walk numbers from titles such as “Walk 42. …” and older titles such as “127. Winter walk …”.

Choose a GeoJSON file to begin.

After conversion: upload the downloaded walks-data.geojson to the root of your walks-map GitHub repository, replacing the previous version. Your GPX files remain untouched.
